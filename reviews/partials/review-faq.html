FAQ

Are these reviews from real customers?

Yes. Only publish reviews you have the right to display. You can manually add feedback from your own customers and update this page over time.

Can customers submit photo reviews?

Yes. You can add photo review entries in the reviews data file and include product or packaging images with each review card.

Can I highlight shipping and communication?

Yes. Use the top mentions section and badge labels to surface common themes like fast shipping, product quality, and support response times.
